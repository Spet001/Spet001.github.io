What is GSC? (Game Scripting C) Guide

Game Scripting C, commonly known as GSC, is the proprietary, server-side scripting language used across almost all major Call of Duty titles powered by the IW engine and Treyarch derivatives. Its syntax is heavily inspired by C and C++, executing on an internal stack-based bytecode virtual machine with multithreading and event notifies.
