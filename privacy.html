Privacy Policy & Cookie Statement

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection & Analytics

This portfolio is hosted as a static website on GitHub Pages and leverages Cloudflare Workers at the edge for backend functionality.

In order to display the "Portfolio Visitors" counter, this site sends an anonymous ping to a Cloudflare Worker upon loading. This function reads non-identifying metadata (such as generic ASN/Country data and User-Agent) solely for the purpose of aggregated analytics and mitigating bot abuse.

2. No Persistent Tracking Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies (e.g., Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) and does not store personally identifiable information (PII) on your device without your explicit consent.

If you interact with the AI Chatbot (SpetBot), your chat preferences may be stored locally on your device via localStorage purely to remember UI states (e.g., whether you dismissed the welcome message).

3. Third-Party Integrations

If you use the "Log in with Patreon" feature to unlock exclusive Journal entries, you will be subject to Patreon's Privacy Policy. The authentication process is handled securely via OAuth 2.0, and your Patreon identity is not persistently stored in our database; it is only used momentarily to verify your subscription status.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding how data is handled on this site, feel free to contact me at gelain15mj@gmail.com.
